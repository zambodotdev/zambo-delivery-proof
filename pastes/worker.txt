Deliver client work with proof on Zambo:
1. Connect to https://zambo.dev/mcp. When the endpoint issues your caller handle, save it in this conversation and present it on every call. You are the worker; the client holds a separate identity of their own.
2. Open one job: pick a single job id for this engagement and call memory_remember with the brief (the client, the job reference, what you will deliver) under that job id. Keep the receipt.
3. Work inside the job. Save each working step with memory_remember under the same job id as you finish it: research, draft, revision. Keep each fact under 500 characters; longer facts are refused. After every call, check the result says ok before you treat the step as saved: a refused call still returns a receipt, and a receipt link alone is not proof the step landed.
4. Wrap the delivery with handover: a summary naming the job, the client, and each step with its receipt; a goal; the client named as the recipient; and next steps telling the client to read the steps and check any receipt at https://zambo.dev/verify/. Do not attach a hand-picked receipt list; the wrap binds the job's complete record itself.
5. Send the client the handover link. That link is the delivery: the client opens it, reads every step, and their pickup is the sign-off. Keep the link and the wrap receipt together as your delivery pack.
